> **Pro feature**

# Share analytics

**WB Plugins > Activity Share > Analytics**

- Totals: shares to other sites, reposts, sends to friends, visits from shared links.
- Shares by network.
- Most shared content, paginated, filterable by activity or posts.
- Period: last 7, 30 or 90 days. **Export CSV** downloads the table.

Reposts and sends are always counted. Clicks and visits are counted only when **Share analytics** is on (Features tab). Visitors are counted with a one-way code that changes daily - IP addresses are never stored. Records are kept 12 months and included in WordPress's personal data export and erase tools.
