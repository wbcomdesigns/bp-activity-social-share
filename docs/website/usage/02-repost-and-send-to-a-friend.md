# Repost or send a post to a friend

> Requires **Activity Share Pro**.

## Quick repost

1. Click **Share** on the post, then **Repost**.
2. The post is reposted to your profile instantly, and a message appears with **Undo** and **View** actions.
3. To remove it later, open the Share menu on the post and choose **Undo repost**.

You cannot repost your own post to your own profile. Use **Repost to a group** for your own posts.

## Repost with a comment

1. Click **Share**, then **Repost with comment**.
2. Under **Where to post**, choose **My profile** or **A group**, then pick the group. **A group** only appears if you belong to one.
3. Optionally write a comment. Type `@` to mention a member - a suggestion list appears as you type.
4. Click **Repost**. The repost shows your comment above a quoted card of the original.

## Repost from inside a group

If the original post belongs to a group, the Share menu offers **Repost in this group** instead. That is always the case for private and hidden groups. For a public group it is the case when the group admin turned off reposting out of the group.

## Send to a friend

1. Click **Share**, then **Send to a friend**.
2. Type a name and pick a friend from the list. Only your friends are shown.
3. Add an optional note and click **Send**. The post arrives as a private BuddyPress message with the title, a short excerpt and up to four pictures, and a **View** link takes you to the conversation.

You must be logged in and, for Repost and Send, follow the community's normal privacy rules - you can only send or repost content you are allowed to read.
