# Privacy and guest sharing

## Visitors can share too

By default, people who are not logged in can share **public** activity - a common request from community owners who want their content to spread. The site owner can turn this off entirely on the [Networks settings](../settings/01-networks.md) screen; when it is off, only logged-in members see the Share button.

## Private content never leaves the community

This rule is fixed and cannot be turned off by a setting:

- Activity inside a **private** or **hidden** group never offers network links, a public copy-link, or a link-preview card to anyone outside the group.
- A reply to a private post follows the same rule as its parent post.
- Hidden and spam activity are never shareable.

Members inside a private or hidden group still see their own in-group sharing options; the restriction is only about sending content to the public internet.

## Why a post has no Share button

If an activity item has no text and is not a repost or blog-post notice (for example "joined the group" or "became friends with"), there is nothing worth sharing, so no Share button shows for it.
