# Share a post

1. Find the activity post, profile or group you want to share.
2. Click the **Share** button.
3. Pick a network (opens a share window with the link and text already filled in), or click **Copy link**.
4. On a phone, tap **More options** to use the phone's own share sheet and pick any installed app.

## Share a blog post or other content

If **Activity Share Pro** is active and content sharing is turned on for a post type, the same Share menu appears on the post itself - above, below the content, or as a floating button, depending on the site's [content type settings](../pro/02-content-types.md). Site owners can also place the menu anywhere with the **Share buttons** block or the `[bpas_share]` shortcode - see the [shortcode and block guide](../developer-guide/03-shortcodes.md).

## Why the menu looks different on a private post

Posts inside private or hidden groups never show network links or a public copy-link, for anyone. See [Privacy and guest sharing](../features/03-privacy-and-guest-sharing.md).
