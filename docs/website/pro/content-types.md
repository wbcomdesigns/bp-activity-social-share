> **Pro feature**

# Share buttons on posts and other content

**WB Plugins > Activity Share > Content types** - tick a content type (posts, pages, products, courses...) and choose where the share menu goes: **below**, **above** the content, or a **floating button**. Default: posts, below.

Place the menu anywhere else with the **Share buttons** block or the `[bpas_share]` shortcode (`[bpas_share id="123"]` for a specific post).

The **Most shared** block and widget list the community posts shared most in the last 7 or 30 days.
