# Activity Share for BuddyPress

Activity Share for BuddyPress adds a **Share** button to every activity post, member profile and public group. Members and visitors can share to Facebook, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and more, copy the link, or use their phone's own share menu.

- Share to Facebook, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Bluesky, Reddit, Pinterest or email.
- Copy the link in one tap.
- On phones, **More options** opens the phone's own share menu (Instagram, Messages and any installed app).
- Visitors can share public posts too - the site owner can turn this off.
- Posts in private and hidden groups never offer sharing to other sites.
- Follows the site's theme, colours and dark mode automatically.

**Start here:** [Requirements](getting-started/01-requirements.md) - [Installation](getting-started/02-installation.md) - [Your first share](getting-started/03-first-share.md)

## Activity Share Pro

**Activity Share Pro** is a paid add-on that extends this free plugin. It adds reposting inside the community, sending posts privately to friends, share buttons on any content type, share counts, analytics, rewards and more. See [Activity Share Pro features](pro/01-features.md).

Free and Pro always ship the same version number and are installed together - Free first, then Pro.
