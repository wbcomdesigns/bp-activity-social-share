# Frequently asked questions

**Can members share to Instagram, TikTok or YouTube?**
Those apps don't accept shared links from websites. On phones, members tap **More options** to open the phone's share menu and pick any installed app.

**Why can't I share to a Facebook Group?**
Facebook's share window decides where a post can go; posting to a group depends on the member's own Facebook permissions.

**Why is there no Share on a post in a private group?**
Only members can see those posts, so a link on another site wouldn't open for anyone else. Members can still copy the link for other members.

**Can I change the word "Share" or move the button?**
Yes - **Appearance** tab.

**How do I stop the link-preview (Open Graph) tags?**
They stop automatically when Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO or SEOPress is active. Otherwise: `add_filter( 'bpas_open_graph_enabled', '__return_false' );`

**BuddyBoss? Youzify?**
BuddyBoss Platform: best effort. Youzify: not supported (it has its own sharing).
