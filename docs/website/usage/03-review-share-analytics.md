# Review your share analytics

> Requires **Activity Share Pro**.

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Activity Share > Features** and turn on **Share analytics** if you also want clicks and visits counted (reposts and sends are always counted).
2. Go to **WB Plugins > Activity Share > Analytics**.
3. Choose a period: **Last 7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**.
4. Optionally filter to **Activity** or **Posts and pages** only.
5. Review the totals (shares to other sites, reposts, sent to friends, visits from shared links), the breakdown by network, and the **Most shared** table.
6. Click **Export CSV** to download the currently visible table.

Analytics records are kept for 12 months by default and are included automatically in WordPress's built-in personal data export and erase tools under **Tools > Export/Erase Personal Data**.
