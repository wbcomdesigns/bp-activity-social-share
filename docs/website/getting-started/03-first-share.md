# Your first share

Once the plugin is active, no setup is required - members and, by default, visitors can start sharing right away.

## Where the Share button appears

- Every activity post in the activity stream, on a profile or inside a group.
- Member profile headers, next to the other profile buttons.
- Public group headers, next to the other group buttons.

## Try it

1. Open your site's activity stream while logged out (or in a private browser window).
2. Find any public activity post and click **Share**.
3. The menu opens with your enabled networks, **Copy link**, and on a phone, **More options** for the phone's own share sheet.
4. Click a network to open its share window, or **Copy link** to copy the URL with a "Link copied" confirmation.

## What is never shareable

Activity inside **private** and **hidden** groups never shows network links or a public copy-link option, for members or visitors. Only "in this group" actions are available there, so private content can never leak outside the community.
