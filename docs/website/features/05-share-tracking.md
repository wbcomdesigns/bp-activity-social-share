# Share counts and tracking

> Requires **Activity Share Pro**.

## Counts and who reposted

When the **Counts and who reposted** feature is on (Features tab), a share count appears next to the Share button once a post has at least one repost, send or (when analytics is on) click. Clicking the count opens a list of the members who reposted the post.

## Share analytics

Turn on **Share analytics** (off by default) to also count clicks on share buttons and visits from shared links, without ever storing an IP address - visitors are counted with a one-way code that changes every day. See [Share analytics](../pro/03-analytics.md) for the full report and CSV export.

Reposts and sends to friends are always counted, even with Share analytics off.

## Short share links

Links shared through the menu use a short form like `yoursite.com/s/abc123` instead of the full activity or post URL. A short link records the visit and credits the member who shared it, without exposing any member ID in the URL itself.

## Share image for text posts

When a text-only post (no image) is shared to Facebook, LinkedIn or WhatsApp, Activity Share Pro generates a branded image card automatically, so the shared link still looks good in the preview.

## Most shared block and widget

The **Most shared** block (and a matching legacy widget) lists the community's most-shared public posts and activity over the last 7 or 30 days. Add it from the block inserter or **Appearance > Widgets**.

## Share rewards

Points for shares that bring a visitor or a new member turn on automatically when **WB Gamification**, **GamiPress** or **myCRED** is active. Point values are set inside that points plugin, not in Activity Share Pro.
