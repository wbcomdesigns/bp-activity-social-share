# Appearance

**WB Plugins > Activity Share > Appearance**

![Appearance settings with live preview](images/appearance.png)

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Button label | Your own text, or leave empty to use "Share" in each visitor's language. |
| Position on activity | After the other buttons (default) or before them. |
| Icon colours | Each network's own colour, or your theme's accent colour. |

The **Preview** is the real menu - open it to check your choices before saving.

The menu follows your theme's colours and dark mode automatically (BuddyX, BuddyX Pro, Reign and most popular themes).
