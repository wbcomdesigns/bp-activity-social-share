# Networks

**WB Plugins > Activity Share > Networks**

![Networks settings](images/networks.png)

## Networks

Tick the networks to show in the share menu. Use the **up / down arrows** to change their order (works with the keyboard too). Defaults: Facebook, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Email, Copy link.

On phones a **More options** button also appears. It opens the phone's own share menu, so members can share to Instagram, TikTok, Messages or any installed app.

## Who and where

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Visitors can share | Shows the share menu to people who are not logged in, on public posts only. |
| Share on member profiles | Adds Share next to the profile header buttons. |
| Share on public groups | Adds Share to public group headers. Private and hidden groups never show outside sharing. |

Click **Save changes**.
