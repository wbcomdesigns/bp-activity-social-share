# Activity Share for BuddyPress

Adds a **Share** button to every activity post, member profile and public group.

- Share to Facebook, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Bluesky, Reddit, Pinterest or email.
- Copy the link in one tap.
- On phones, **More options** opens the phone's own share menu (Instagram, Messages and any installed app).
- Visitors can share public posts too.
- Posts in private and hidden groups never offer sharing to other sites.

**Start here:** [Installation](getting-started/installation.md) - [Networks](settings/networks.md) - [Appearance](settings/appearance.md) - [FAQ](faq.md)

**Activity Share Pro** adds reposting, sending to friends, share buttons on any content, counts, trending posts, rewards and analytics: [see Pro features](pro/features.md).
