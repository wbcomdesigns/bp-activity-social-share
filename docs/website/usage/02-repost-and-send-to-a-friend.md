# Repost or send a post to a friend

> Requires **Activity Share Pro**.

## Quick repost

1. Click **Share** on the post, then **Repost**.
2. The post is reposted to your profile instantly, and a toast appears with an **Undo** action.
3. To remove it later, open the Share menu on the repost and choose **Undo repost**.

## Repost with a comment

1. Click **Share**, then **Repost with comment**.
2. Write your comment in the composer. Type `@` to mention a member - a suggestion list appears as you type.
3. Choose the destination: your own profile, or a group you belong to.
4. Click **Post**. The repost shows your comment above a quoted card of the original.

## Repost from inside a group

If the original post belongs to a group, the Share menu offers **Repost in this group** instead, unless the group admin has allowed sharing outside the group.

## Send to a friend

1. Click **Share**, then **Send to a friend**.
2. Pick a friend from the list (searchable once you have more than a few).
3. Add an optional note and click **Send**. The post arrives as a private BuddyPress message.

You must be logged in and, for Repost and Send, follow the community's normal privacy rules - you can only send or repost content you are allowed to read.
